Members of the **GO-EUREKA** network took part in the [AOGS 2026 Annual Meeting](https://www.asiaoceania.org/AOGS2026/Home), held **2–7 August 2026** in **Fukuoka, Japan**, under the theme *"Science Horizons, Pacific Synergy."* Across the week, the team delivered **six presentations** — one invited oral, three contributed orals, and two posters — sharing the latest results on detecting earthquakes and tsunamis from ionospheric GNSS observations.
