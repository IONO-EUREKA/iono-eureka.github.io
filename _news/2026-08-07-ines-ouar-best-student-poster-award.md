We are delighted to announce that **Ines Dahlia Ouar**, PhD student within the GO-EUREKA network, is a winner of the **AOGS 2026 Best Student Poster Award**! The award was presented at the [AOGS 2026 Annual Meeting](https://www.asiaoceania.org/AOGS2026/Home) in Fukuoka, Japan, recognizing outstanding student research from among the meeting's many poster contributions.

*Ines Ouar receiving her Best Student Poster Award certificate at the AOGS 2026 closing ceremony in Fukuoka, Japan.*

### The Winning Poster

Ines was recognized for her poster:

**Near Real Time Detection and Localization of Earthquakes from the Ionosphere**
I.D. Ouar, Q. Brissaud, E. Astafyeva, P. Inchin
[View abstract →](https://www.asiaoceania.org/AOGS2026/Program-Details/39579/near-real-time-detection-and-localization-of-earthquakes-from-the-ionosphere)

The work presents an approach for detecting and localizing earthquakes in near real time from their signature in ionospheric Total Electron Content (TEC), a core building block of the near-real-time monitoring capability GO-EUREKA is developing for earthquake and tsunami early warning.

See the [full list of 2026 AOGS Best Student Poster Award winners](https://www.asiaoceania.org/society/public.asp?page=best_student_poster_2026.asp#IG) for the complete competition results.

This award is a fantastic recognition of Ines's research and a great moment for the whole GO-EUREKA network. Well done, Ines — we're proud to have you on the team!
